ML-KEM on three GPUs, up to 55× faster than CPU

My M.Tech thesis at IISc (SINESYS Lab, Prof. Utsav Banerjee) implements NIST FIPS 203 ML-KEM KeyGen and Encaps as an end-to-end CUDA pipeline with standard-conformant encodings.

The speed comes from Tensor Core NTTs, a PTX-level Keccak that uses lop3.b32 for the χ step, device-side byte-splitting and async streams. I also analysed where the pipeline leaks timing and memory-access information, and specified the countermeasures.
